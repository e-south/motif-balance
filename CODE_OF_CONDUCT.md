# Code of conduct

Motif Balance contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
harassment-free, and constructive regardless of identity, background,
experience, or viewpoint.

Acceptable participation includes specific technical feedback, clear evidence,
respect for boundaries, and good-faith correction. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation
when conduct threatens the project or its community. Report concerns privately
through the repository security-advisory interface. Reports will be handled
with confidentiality proportional to the circumstances.

This policy follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
